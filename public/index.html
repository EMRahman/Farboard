Games go through a .

A relay is the small server that passes moves between the two players. This one runs on the free Cloudflare plan of whoever runs this site, and everyone using the site shares it, so it allows a set number of new games each day. Moves are end-to-end encrypted: the relay passes them along but cannot read them. A copy of your own comes with a relay of your own and no daily limit.

    Play online

    Your own copy is the whole of Farboard, this page and a relay, on your own free Cloudflare account at an address of your own. People you invite need nothing. You do this once.

    1. Copy an owner key

      It lets you start games on your copy. Keep it private; the people you invite never need it.

    2. Deploy to Cloudflare

      Sign in to Cloudflare and GitHub, or create free accounts, when asked. When Cloudflare asks for OWNER_KEY, paste the key from step 1.

      Deploy to Cloudflare ↗

    3. Open your copy

      Paste the address Cloudflare gives you, for example farboard.yourname.workers.dev. Your key goes with you.

    The owner key lets you start games on a relay you run: always on a private relay, and past the daily limit on a public one.
